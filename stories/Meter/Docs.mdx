# Meter

A segmented progress bar that shows values against a maximum, such as spend
against a limit, quota usage or capacity. Segments stack left to right; when
their total exceeds the maximum the overflow renders in the error colour.

<Canvas of={MeterStories.Default} />

## With a marker

A marker sits at a fraction of the bar (0–1) and shows a tooltip on hover
or focus. Use it to mark a point like "Today" along a time-based bar.

<Canvas of={MeterStories.WithMarker} />

## Over limit

When segments sum past `max`, the portion beyond the limit renders in the
error colour. The bar never overflows its container.

<Canvas of={MeterStories.OverLimit} />

## Multiple segments

Any number of segments can be stacked. Each needs a `label` for the
generated accessible name. Add `pattern: 'striped'` to make a segment
distinguishable without colour.

<Canvas of={MeterStories.MultipleSegments} />

## Accessibility

The component renders with `role="img"`. Without a supplied `aria-label`,
one is generated from the segments, the maximum and the remaining amount,
for example: "€1,100 Approved, €100 Pending, of a €1,250 limit. €50 left."

Pass `formatValue` to control how numbers appear in the generated label.

Under `forced-colors: active`, solid segments use `CanvasText`, striped
segments use a pattern of `CanvasText` and `Canvas`, and the track has a
visible border.
